# PTIL — Letter of Credit (LC) Handling Process

**Department:** Finance / Commercial
**Applies To:** Export order fulfillment

## Overview

Precision Textile Industry Ltd exports finished garments and fabric rolls to
buyers primarily in Europe and North America. Most export orders are settled
via an irrevocable Letter of Credit (LC) opened by the buyer's bank in favor
of PTIL.

## LC Lifecycle

1. **LC Receipt**: Commercial team receives the LC copy from the buyer's
   bank via the negotiating bank, and logs it in the LC tracking sheet with
   LC number, issuing bank, amount, expiry date, and shipment deadline.
2. **Document Preparation**: Commercial and Finance jointly prepare the
   required shipping documents — commercial invoice, packing list, bill of
   lading, certificate of origin, and inspection certificate — ensuring
   strict compliance with LC terms (any discrepancy risks payment delay or
   rejection).
3. **Bank Negotiation**: Documents are submitted to the negotiating bank
   within the LC's document presentation period (typically 21 days after
   shipment).
4. **Payment Realization**: Upon acceptance, payment is credited to PTIL's
   bank account, usually 5–7 banking days after document acceptance for
   sight LCs, or per the deferred payment terms for usance LCs.

## Common Discrepancies to Avoid

- Shipment date on the Bill of Lading later than the LC's latest shipment
  date.
- Invoice amount exceeding the LC value.
- Mismatched consignee details between LC and shipping documents.
- Late presentation of documents beyond the allowed window.

## Escalation

Any LC nearing expiry within 5 days without shipment readiness is escalated
immediately to the Commercial Manager and Factory Manager to expedite
production or negotiate an amendment with the buyer.
