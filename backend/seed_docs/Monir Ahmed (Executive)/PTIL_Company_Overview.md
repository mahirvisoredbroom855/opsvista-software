# Precision Textile Industry Limited (PTIL) — Company Overview

**Document Type:** Corporate Reference
**Owner:** Executive Office
**Classification:** Internal
**Last Reviewed:** September 2026

This document is the primary reference for questions about who PTIL is, how
it is owned and structured, what it does, and how its departments fit
together. Department-specific policies, logs, and financial records live in
their own folders — this document exists so anyone (or the Knowledge
Assistant) can answer basic "who/what/how does the company work" questions
without having to piece it together from operational files.

## Company Identity

- **Legal name:** Precision Textile Industry Limited
- **Trade name:** PTIL
- **Industry:** Textile manufacturing — specialized printing and embroidery
  services for garment brands and buying houses
- **Headquarters / sole production facility:** Gazipur, Bangladesh
- **Ownership:** Privately held. **Monir Ahmed** is the Owner and final
  decision-maker across all departments; department heads listed below
  report directly to the Owner.
- **Incorporation:** Registered under the Bangladesh Companies Act; operates
  in compliance with the **Bangladesh Labour Act 2006** (referenced
  throughout HR policy, e.g. maternity leave entitlement).

## What PTIL Does

PTIL does not manufacture garments from scratch — it provides **value-added
textile services** to garment brands and buying houses who send in blank or
partially finished fabric/garments for:

- **Printing** — screen printing and digital printing on woven and knit
  fabric
- **Embroidery** — including MHM (multi-head machine) embroidery for
  higher-volume, multi-color designs
- Supporting sample development and approval before bulk production
  (see the Commercial department's Sample Approval Tracker)

Production runs are organized by **style reference** (e.g. `PTIL-WV-118` for
a woven style, `PTIL-KN-430` for a knit style) and tracked from order
release through delivery.

### Known buyers / clients

Recurring buyer names that appear across commercial and order records
include **Alpine Textiles Co.**, **NordicWear AB**, and **Heritage Apparel
Inc.** — international buying houses that place recurring orders across
multiple style references.

Production floor lines are split by process: **knitting**, **weaving**,
**printing**, and **embroidery** (including the MHM embroidery line
referenced in maintenance and order-delay records) — these are shared
infrastructure that Commercial, Maintenance, and Finance all reference from
their respective angles (order feasibility, machine capacity, and cost).
