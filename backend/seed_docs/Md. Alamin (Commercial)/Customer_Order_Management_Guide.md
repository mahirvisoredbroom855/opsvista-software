# PTIL — Customer Order Management Guide

**Department:** Commercial
**Owner:** Assistant Commercial Manager (Md. Alamin)

## Order Intake

All new export and domestic orders are logged in the Order Tracking sheet
with buyer name, style/reference number, quantity, agreed price (per piece
or per kg), delivery deadline, and payment terms (LC, TT, or open account).

## Order Confirmation Workflow

1. Buyer sends purchase order (PO) or proforma invoice request.
2. Commercial team checks production capacity with the Factory Manager
   before confirming delivery timeline.
3. Pricing is validated against current raw material costs (yarn, dye,
   accessories) provided by the Finance team.
4. Confirmed orders are entered into the production planning schedule.

## Sample Approval Process

For new buyers or new styles, a pre-production sample (PP sample) must be
approved by the buyer before bulk production begins. Sample approval
turnaround target: 5 working days from sample dispatch.

## Delivery & Shipment Coordination

- Domestic orders: coordinated with the local logistics team, delivery
  confirmation logged same-day.
- Export orders: coordinated with freight forwarders; shipment booking
  must be confirmed at least 7 days before the vessel's cutoff date to
  avoid rolled cargo.

## Customer Communication Standard

All buyer correspondence regarding order status, delays, or quality issues
must be acknowledged within 24 hours, even if a full resolution is still
pending. Delayed orders (more than 3 days past agreed deadline) require a
proactive notification to the buyer with a revised delivery date.
