# PTIL — Machine Maintenance Schedule & Safety SOP

**Department:** Maintenance
**Owner:** Maintenance Manager (Khorshed Alam Babu)

## Preventive Maintenance Schedule

| Machine Type | Inspection Frequency | Full Service Interval |
|---|---|---|
| Circular Knitting Machines | Weekly | Every 3 months |
| Dyeing Machines | Weekly | Every 2 months |
| Sewing/Production Lines | Daily (operator check) | Monthly |
| Generators (backup power) | Daily (load test) | Every 6 months |
| Boilers | Daily (pressure check) | Every 6 months (certified inspection) |

## Daily Checklist (Production Floor)

1. Operator performs a visual safety check before starting any machine.
2. Any unusual noise, vibration, or smell is reported immediately to the
   floor supervisor and logged in the Machine Issue Log.
3. Machines flagged for repair are tagged "Out of Service" and physically
   isolated from power until repaired.

## Breakdown Response

- **Critical machines** (affecting active production orders): response
  target of 30 minutes from report to maintenance technician arrival.
  Downtime is logged with start time, cause, and resolution time for the
  monthly performance report.
- **Non-critical machines**: response target of 4 hours.

## Safety Requirements

- All maintenance technicians must wear PPE (gloves, safety glasses, and
  ear protection where applicable) before servicing any machine.
- Lockout-tagout (LOTO) procedure is mandatory before performing internal
  repairs on any powered equipment.
- New employees assigned to production floors must complete machine safety
  induction (see HR Onboarding Policy) before operating any equipment.

## Spare Parts Inventory

Critical spare parts (needles, belts, motors, control boards) are
maintained in the Maintenance store with a minimum stock threshold. Falling
below threshold triggers a reorder alert to the Admin/Procurement team.
