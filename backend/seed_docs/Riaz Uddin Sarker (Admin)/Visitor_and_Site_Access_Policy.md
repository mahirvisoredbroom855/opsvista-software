# PTIL — Visitor and Site Access Policy

**Department:** Administration
**Owner:** Admin Manager (Riaz Uddin Sarker)

## Purpose

Governs access control for the Gazipur factory site, covering employees,
contractors, buyers, auditors, and general visitors.

## Visitor Registration

All visitors must register at the main gate security desk, presenting a
valid photo ID. The security guard logs the visitor's name, company,
purpose of visit, host employee, and time of entry/exit in the Visitor
Register. Visitors are issued a temporary badge that must be worn visibly
at all times on-site.

## Categories of Access

- **General Visitors**: Escorted access to head office and meeting rooms
  only. No unescorted factory floor access.
- **Buyer / Auditor Visits**: Pre-scheduled at least 24 hours in advance
  through the Commercial or Compliance team. Factory floor access requires
  safety briefing and PPE (personal protective equipment).
- **Contractors / Vendors**: Access limited to the specific work area,
  supervised by the Maintenance team, and logged separately in the
  Contractor Access Log.

## Security & CCTV

The facility maintains CCTV coverage across production floors, warehouse,
and main entry points for security and productivity monitoring purposes.
Footage is retained for 60 days per the data retention policy before
automated purge.

## Asset Management

All company assets (laptops, machinery tools, factory equipment) issued to
employees must be recorded in the Asset Register with a unique asset tag.
Asset returns upon employee exit are verified by Admin before the final
settlement is processed by HR and Finance.

## Emergency Procedures

Fire evacuation drills are conducted quarterly. Assembly points are marked
at the front yard and rear loading dock. The Admin team maintains an
updated emergency contact list posted at each floor's notice board.
