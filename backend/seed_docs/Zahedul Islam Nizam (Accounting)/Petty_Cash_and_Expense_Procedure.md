# PTIL — Petty Cash and Expense Reimbursement Procedure

**Department:** Accounting
**Owner:** Accounting Assistant (Zahedul Islam Nizam)

## Petty Cash Fund

Each department (HR, Admin, Maintenance, Commercial) maintains a petty cash
float of ৳10,000, replenished monthly upon submission of receipts and an
expense summary sheet.

## Expense Categories

- Office supplies and stationery
- Local transport and courier charges
- Minor repairs and maintenance materials (under ৳2,000)
- Refreshments for buyer/auditor visits

## Reimbursement Process

1. Employee submits original receipts with a filled expense claim form
   within 7 days of the expense.
2. Department head countersigns the claim.
3. Accounting verifies receipts against the claim amount and processes
   reimbursement within 3 working days via cash or bank transfer.
4. All petty cash transactions are recorded in the Cash Book workbook under
   the appropriate department tag, feeding into the daily finance
   reconciliation process.

## Expense Claims Above ৳5,000

Any single expense claim exceeding ৳5,000 requires prior written approval
from the Commercial Manager before the expense is incurred, and cannot be
reimbursed retroactively without an approved exception form.

## Audit Trail

All petty cash disbursements and replenishments are logged with date,
amount, purpose, department, and approver name to support the monthly
finance audit and the company's broader financial governance requirements.
