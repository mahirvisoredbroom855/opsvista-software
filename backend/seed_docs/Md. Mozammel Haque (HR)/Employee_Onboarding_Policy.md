# Precision Textile Industry LTD — Employee Onboarding Policy

**Department:** Human Resources
**Effective Date:** January 2025
**Location:** Gazipur, Dhaka, Bangladesh

## Purpose

This policy defines the standard onboarding process for all new employees joining
Precision Textile Industry Ltd (PTIL), covering factory floor staff, production
supervisors, and head office personnel.

## Onboarding Timeline

1. **Day 1 — Orientation**: New hires report to the HR office at 9:00 AM for
   document verification (NID, education certificates, prior employment
   references). HR issues an employee ID card and factory access badge.
2. **Day 1–3 — Safety Induction**: All production-floor employees must complete
   the mandatory fire safety and machine safety induction conducted by the
   Maintenance department before being allowed on the factory floor.
3. **Week 1 — Departmental Shadowing**: New employees shadow a senior team
   member in their assigned department (Finance, Commercial, Production, or
   Admin) to learn daily workflows.
4. **Day 30 — Probation Review**: Direct manager submits a probation review
   form to HR. Employees on probation receive 80% of base salary until
   confirmation.
5. **Day 90 — Confirmation**: Upon successful probation review, HR issues a
   confirmation letter and updates the employee's status in the HR system.

## Required Documents

- National ID (NID) or passport copy
- Educational certificates (attested copies)
- Two passport-size photographs
- Bank account details for salary disbursement
- Emergency contact information

## Roles Involved

- **HR Manager (Md. Mozammel Haque)**: Owns the onboarding checklist and
  confirms employee records in the HR system.
- **Factory Manager**: Approves floor placement for production staff.
- **Maintenance Team**: Conducts safety induction training.

## Key Performance Indicator

New employee onboarding should be completed (documents verified, ID issued,
safety induction passed) within **3 working days** of the start date.
