# PTIL — Leave and Attendance Policy

**Department:** Human Resources
**Applies To:** All permanent employees

## Leave Categories

| Leave Type | Annual Entitlement | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Casual Leave | 10 days | Requires 1 day advance notice |
| Sick Leave | 14 days | Medical certificate required beyond 3 consecutive days |
| Earned Leave | 18 days | Accrues monthly, can be carried forward up to 30 days |
| Festival Leave | 2 occasions/year | Aligned with Eid and other national holidays |
| Maternity Leave | 16 weeks | As per Bangladesh Labour Act 2006 |

## Attendance Tracking

Attendance for factory-floor staff is recorded via fingerprint biometric
scanners installed at each production floor entrance. Head office staff use
a badge-swipe system at the main gate. Attendance data feeds into the HR
dashboard within 60 seconds of each punch, per the Real-Time HR Updates
initiative.

## Late Arrival Policy

- 0–15 minutes late: recorded, no penalty for first 3 occurrences per month.
- 16–60 minutes late: half-day casual leave deduction.
- More than 60 minutes late without prior approval: full-day leave deduction
  and note in the employee file.

## Leave Request Process

1. Employee submits leave request through the HR portal or, for factory
   floor staff without portal access, via a paper form countersigned by the
   line supervisor.
2. Direct manager approves or rejects within 2 working days.
3. HR updates the attendance/leave ledger and notifies payroll before the
   monthly cutoff (25th of each month).

## Escalation

Unapproved absences exceeding 3 consecutive days without communication are
escalated to the HR Manager and may result in disciplinary action per the
Employee Code of Conduct.
